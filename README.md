## Engineering at Virtue

This GitHub organisation is home to software, automation, integrations and operational tooling developed by Virtue Technologies.

Much of our engineering work supports customer environments, managed services and commercial platforms, so many repositories are private by design. Public projects and reusable resources will appear here where they can offer genuine value to the wider education and technology communities.

Our engineering priorities are straightforward:

- **Secure by design** - protecting data, identities and tenant boundaries from the start
- **Operationally useful** - solving real problems for technical teams
- **Reliable in production** - observable, maintainable and built for long-term use
- **Clear and accountable** - documented decisions, controlled change and visible outcomes
- **Education aware** - technology shaped around the realities of schools and Trusts
